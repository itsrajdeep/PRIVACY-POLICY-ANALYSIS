STEP 2: PRIVACY POLICY EXTRACTION
================================================================================

Tool Used: trafilatura (Python library)
Script: process_policies.py

Process:
  1. Read companies_privacy_policies.csv
  2. Filter out rows with missing URLs (2 removed: Credit Suisse, InVision)
  3. For each company, download the privacy policy page using trafilatura.fetch_url()
  4. Extract text content using trafilatura.extract()
  5. Reject policies with fewer than 500 words (too short to be real policies)
  6. Save results to dataset/privacy_policies.csv
  7. Save raw text files to dataset/raw_text/{company}.txt

Results:
  - Successfully extracted: 81 policies
  - Failed: 67 companies (download failures or text too short)

STEP 4: FEATURE ENGINEERING
================================================================================

File: dataset/policy_features.csv
- 81 rows, one per company
- 9 feature columns (described below)

Script: generate_features.py
- Reads from dataset/privacy_policies.csv
- Computes additional features (unique_words, readability scores, legal jargon)
- Outputs to dataset/policy_features.csv

Feature Dictionary:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Feature               | Meaning              | Description
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  word_count            | Length                | Total number of words in policy
  sentence_count        | Complexity            | Total number of sentences
  avg_sentence_length   | Readability           | Words per sentence (average)
  char_count            | Size                  | Total character count
  unique_words          | Vocabulary Richness   | Number of distinct words used
  flesch_reading_ease   | Readability Score     | Higher = easier to read
                        |                       |   90-100: Very easy (5th grade)
                        |                       |   60-70:  Standard
                        |                       |   30-50:  Difficult
                        |                       |   0-30:   Very confusing
  flesch_kincaid_grade  | Grade Level           | US school grade level needed
  legal_term_count      | Jargon Density        | Count of legal terms found
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

STEP 5: READABILITY SCORES
